04 — HOW I THINK

Four things I believe
that show up in the work.

tap or hover a card to flip →

01

Strategy that can't survive contact with the frontline isn't strategy.

It's a PowerPoint.

The best test of a strategy isn't the exec approval — it's whether a sales rep or ops manager can act on it Monday morning without asking three questions. Everything else is a whiteboard exercise.

02

The right answer is usually a mile away from the loudest voice in the room.

Data is a great equalizer.

I've seen $70M growth opportunities missed because a senior VP was sure about a customer segment. Numbers don't care about tenure. My job is often to bring the number, translate what it means, and stay in the room while everyone re-adjusts.

03

Pilots beat plans. Every time.

Measure. Then scale.

I default to small operational pilots before big rollouts. Not because I'm risk-averse — because a two-week pilot with real customers tells you more than a two-month planning cycle. The cost of a small failure is the tuition for a big win.

04

Partnerships are people, not org charts.

You either build the relationship or you don't have one.

Every partner motion I've shipped came down to one thing: a shared calendar, a shared metric, and enough trust to disagree in public. The rest is choreography.
