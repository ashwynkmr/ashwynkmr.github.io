01 · THE DNA

Nine years,
three continents,
one obsession.

I started at a Bangalore ed-tech startup. Eleven people, one Google Sheet for a CRM, and a lot of ambition. Nine years later, I'm at Dell as the cybersecurity partner strategist for our global software portfolio. Translation: I stand between five business units, three product teams, and vendors with strong opinions, and make the motion actually ship. AI is how I keep up. Cross-functional problem solving is how I keep score.

In between, four years at ZS Associates on high-stakes engagements with Fortune 50 pharma, health insurance, and specialty pharmacy clients. The kind of engagements where the deck lands at 6:47 for a 7:00 client call and you're still calm. Six of us broke into US health insurance from scratch. I built the client SME and dataset depth, then trained every senior and junior hire that followed. Six became sixty. Client management, executive communication, quantitative rigor, structured problem solving. The go-hard toolkit got hammered in.

Different industries, different scales, different rooms. Same pull. A hard, tangled problem, and a room full of people I can learn from while we untangle it.

Today, the problem solving happens with AI in the loop. At work, I use it to compress days of analysis into hours. At home, I build with it. I don't wait for a strategy deck to know if something will work; I prototype it, put it in front of real people, then write the story around what I learned.

the best problems are the ones that teach you something new.
